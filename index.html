About

I've spent 10+ years in talent acquisition and HR ops. Most of that time I've been the person who automates the manual stuff, untangles the broken workflows, and makes the data actually usable — not just technically present.

Clarity Loop is how I work with teams outside my day job. I'm based in the Philippines and work with clients remotely. I don't take on everything — I take on things I can actually solve well.

What I do
Recruitment ops and ATS setup
+
Getting your hiring process to actually run the way it's supposed to — clean stages, consistent data, no more chasing people for updates. I've worked in SmartRecruiters, Greenhouse, and Airtable, and I've migrated systems with 10k+ candidate profiles without losing anyone's data.
Workflow automation
+
If something in your process is done manually every single time, it probably shouldn't be. I build automations using Google Apps Script, Zapier, and API integrations — not just connect-the-dots stuff, but logic that handles edge cases and doesn't break when someone does something unexpected.
People analytics and dashboards
+
Recruitment data that sits in your ATS and never gets looked at is just noise. I turn it into hiring funnel visibility, time-to-fill tracking, and numbers that actually help you make decisions. Looker Studio, Metabase, or whatever you're already using.
